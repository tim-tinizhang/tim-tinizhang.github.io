I am a final-year undergraduate student in Government and Public Administration at the University of Macau.

My research interests lie in development economics, political economy, labor economics, and international economics, with a regional focus on China and East Asia.

Broadly, I study how global economic integration, domestic institutions, and development strategies shape production, distribution, and social competition.

One strand of my current work examines how cross-border narratives and information reshape distributional beliefs and preferences over labor protection.
